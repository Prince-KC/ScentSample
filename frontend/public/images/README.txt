You can place your perfume images in these folders:

1. Middle Eastern:
   frontend/public/images/middle-eastern/
   Reference in app as: "/images/middle-eastern/your-image.jpg"

2. Designer:
   frontend/public/images/designer/
   Reference in app as: "/images/designer/your-image.jpg"

3. Niche:
   frontend/public/images/niche/
   Reference in app as: "/images/niche/your-image.jpg"

4. Hero & Banners:
   frontend/public/images/hero/
   Reference in app as: "/images/hero/your-image.jpg"
